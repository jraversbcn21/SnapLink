# Privacy Policy for SnapLink

## What data we collect

Nothing. SnapLink does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data, browsing data, or any data of any kind.

The extension reads the title and URL of the page you are currently viewing, and only when you explicitly trigger it — by clicking the toolbar icon or pressing the keyboard shortcut. This information is used solely to build the hyperlink that gets copied to your clipboard. It never leaves your browser.

## How the extension works

SnapLink operates entirely inside your browser:

- It reads the page title and URL from the DOM of the active tab.
- It writes a formatted hyperlink (HTML and plain text) to your system clipboard.
- It injects a small toast notification on the page to confirm the copy.

No network requests are made. No data is sent anywhere. Everything happens locally on your device.

## Third parties

SnapLink does not integrate with any third-party services, APIs, analytics platforms, or advertising networks. Your data is never shared with anyone because it is never collected in the first place.

## User rights

Since SnapLink collects zero data, there is nothing to access, delete, or port. You have full control — the extension only does something when you choose to use it.

## Changes to this policy

If the extension ever changes in a way that affects data collection or privacy, this policy will be updated and the changes will be reflected in the version history of the extension's repository.
